## CAN-USB

A USB-C adapter that connects a computer to CAN and CAN FD networks. On Linux it works with the SocketCAN `gs_usb` driver.

- Three CAN channels: CAN 2.0B up to 1 Mbit/s, CAN FD up to 8 Mbit/s
- USB 2.0 High Speed over USB-C, powered from USB
- Software-selectable termination
- D-Sub 9-pin connector (CiA 106)
